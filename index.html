his story

Serene comes from Selangor, and I (Ravin) am from Penang. 

We met at church in 2024. I took a liking towards her, and she fell for me. 

Nervously, I pursued her. One day, I offered to fix her laptop, then took her out for ramen. 

Later, Serene asked me out to jog together, and soon we were spending time together often.

In 2025, we met late one night at a mamak shop and decided to be together. 

It was exciting, our very first love.

"I have found the one whom my soul loves." (Song of Solomon 3:4)

her story

He is always there whenever I (Serene) need him. 

Even when he is exhausted. making a tired "eeeya" sound as he lifts me, he still carries me. 

He makes sure I am completely okay so I can stand up and run again. 

He does the same for my spiritual life, making sure I never stray from God, and encouraging me to press on and finish the race well.

Through every season of life, the moments of joy, hardships, and ups and down, we have walked side by side. 

We watched each other grow, faced every challenges together, and always held each other's hand.
